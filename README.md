# Employee-Survey-Analysis
Analysis of an employee engagement survey using Excel and pivot tables to uncover agreement trends by question, department, and role.

## Project OverView
An analysis of an employee engagement survey conducted by Pierce County, WA, examining agreement trends across survey questions, departments, and roles. Built in Excel using pivot tables, pivot charts, and conditional formatting. Dataset sourced from Maven Analytics' Data Playground.

## Analysis Questions

1. Which survey questions did respondents agree with or disagree with most?
2. Do you see any patterns or trends by department or role?
3. As an employer, what steps might you take to improve employee satisfaction based on the survey results?

## Key Findings 
1. Question 1, "I know what is expected of me at work," had the highest agreement at 91.16%. Question 6, "I have a best friend at work," had the highest disagreement at 41.20%, followed by the recognition and praise question at 33.65%.

2. Question 6 is the lowest-agreement question in nearly every department, out of 21, and across all four roles, showing this is an organisation-wide issue rather than isolated to one team. Exceptions: the Sheriff's Department and Clerks of Superior Courts were weakest on the recognition and praise question instead, and Assigned Council was weakest on supervisor accountability. By role, Supervisor had the highest agreement rate at 80.54%, though Staff and Supervisor are the largest respondent groups by raw count, and Managers specifically rated supervisor accountability lowest among their own top concerns.

3. Recommended actions: build structured teamwork, cross-functional projects, and team social events to address the workplace-friendship gap; introduce a regular, structured recognition process built into one-to-ones to address the praise gap; and standardise performance-review practices so accountability is applied consistently across supervisors.
